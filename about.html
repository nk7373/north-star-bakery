About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened as a small family storefront on Maple Street, with one oven and a handful of recipes passed down through three generations. It's grown into a neighborhood gathering spot since then, and we still bake in the same small batches we started with.

Sourcing Philosophy

We buy our flour from a mill less than fifty miles away and work with local dairies and orchards whenever a recipe calls for it. We also keep the menu fairly small on purpose, since it's easier to do a few things well.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker, Lena, has been with the bakery for over a decade and still bakes the first loaf of the day herself. Our weekend team is mostly students from the neighborhood who grew up stopping in after school.
