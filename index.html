Welcome to North Star Bakery

Welcome

North Star Bakery has been baking fresh bread and pastries for the neighborhood every morning since the day we opened our doors. A lot of our regulars stop in on their commute, and we also take orders for family gatherings and events.

What We're Known For

Our ovens run before sunrise so that every loaf, pastry, and cake is made fresh the same day it's sold. We source our flour from a local mill and bake everything in small batches.

Hours and Location

Open Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are located at 212 Maple Street, just two blocks from the train station.
